Overview

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a university in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially chartered on 1 February 2007. UMT began as the Centre of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) located at Mengabang Telipot, Terengganu.

It provides facilities for both students and lecturers from the agriculture and fisheries programs to conduct practical training and research. Later, it was upgraded to a branch campus and renamed Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). UMT is well known for its focus on oceanography and marine sciences.
